Limits

How to read this

  • Counts come from AIS signals. Ships that switch off or spoof their transponder ("dark" ships) are missed or misplaced, so the true number of passages can be higher than shown.
  • Data arrives late. IMF PortWatch publishes weekly, on Tuesdays around 9 AM New York time, and the latest days can be revised. Every count on this page shows the day it refers to.
  • A claim is not a confirmation. "Claimed only" means one party said it; it is not evidence that it happened. Each row names who said it and links the source.
  • The forecast is a range. Real counts fall outside the shaded band about one day in five, more often when events move fast.
  • Rules can change. Settlement rules are copied by hand and dated. Always re-read the venue's own text.
